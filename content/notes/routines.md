## Supplements

I take supplements only when the evidence is strong or moderate.

| Supplement           | Dose                                       | Why                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Vitamin B12          | 350 µg/day                                 | Essential on a vegan diet. Strong evidence.                         |
| Iodine               | 200 µg every other day (potassium iodide)  | Vegan diets are often low in iodine. Strong evidence.               |
| Creatine monohydrate | 5 g/day (capsules)                         | Muscle and strength. Vegans start with less of it. Strong evidence. |
| Vitamin D3 (vegan)   | 2,000 IU/day Oct–Mar, 1,000 IU/day Apr–Sep | Little sun in German winters. Moderate evidence.                    |
| Algal oil            | 1 capsule/day (265 mg EPA+DHA)             | Vegan source of EPA and DHA. Moderate evidence.                     |
| Zinc + selenium      | 15 mg zinc + 100 µg selenium               | Pending upcoming blood test                                         |

Not taken:
- Magnesium, calcium and iron: my food covers them.
- L-citrulline and maca: weak evidence.

## Teeth

- Brush three times if i can, at least twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste (1450 ppm).
- I bring a toothbrush with me usually.
- I use two different toothpastes: a whitening one in the morning and an antibacterial one in the evening. Both have fluoride.
  - _Evidence:_ I found no study showing that two toothpastes work better than one. The evidence is for fluoride at 1450 ppm, twice a day. A whitening toothpaste removes some surface stains. It does not change the colour of the tooth.
- Brush in the morning **before** eating.
  - _Evidence:_ this is reasonable. Acidic food softens the enamel for a short time, so brushing straight after eating can wear it. Fluoride on the teeth before a meal also helps. My first meal is at 12:00, so this is easy.
- After brushing, spit out the toothpaste but don't rinse with water. The fluoride then stays on the teeth.

## Hearing

- **Concert earplugs:** custom-made, with filters, from Hörluchs. They lower the volume evenly, so music still sounds clear.
  - _Evidence:_ a randomised trial at a music festival found less temporary hearing loss with earplugs than without (Ramakers et al., JAMA Otolaryngology, 2016).
