i've taken the [10% pledge](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/pledge). \
this means i donate 10% of my income every month to effective charities \
i recommend donating to [giving what we can](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/), \
[effektiv spenden](https://effektiv-spenden.org), or [give well](https://www.givewell.org/). \
i donate to effektiv spenden because it's tax deductible for me. \
before i took the pledge i donated a variable % of my income monthly and individual small donations. \
saving one life is estimated to cost between \$3,000 and \$5,500. \
i proudly say that i have probably saved one human life with my donations.

other places that probably make sense to donate to are: [EA](https://www.every.org/centre-for-effective-altruism-inc), [80k Hours](https://80000hours.org/support-us/donate/), [miri](https://intelligence.org/donate/) but
[tetraspace has a blurb about why donor lotteries are good](https://thetetra.space/effective-altruism/) and i encourage you to donate to them.
