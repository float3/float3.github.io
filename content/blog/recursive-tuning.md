I talked about [recursive just intonation](https://hilll.dev/blog/recursive-just-intonation/) in the past, but I know realize that that's just a specific instance of a more general type of adaptive tuningsystem I've discovered which I call "Recursive Tuning Systems" or just "Recursive Tuning".

in this post you'll be able to make a matrix of any two tuningsystems that are supported by my music21-rs library and see how they interact with each other and how they sound.
